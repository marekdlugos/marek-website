{{- /* Flattens Hugo's nested .Fragments.Headings into a plain slice of {id, level, title}, dropping the empty placeholder entries Hugo inserts for heading levels a post skips. Recursive: it calls itself for children. Returns: slice of dicts. Call with a .Fragments.Headings value. */ -}} {{- $out := slice -}} {{- range . -}} {{- $h := . -}} {{- with $h.Title -}} {{- $out = $out | append (dict "id" $h.ID "level" $h.Level "title" .) -}} {{- end -}} {{- with $h.Headings -}} {{- range partial "toc-headings.html" . -}} {{- $out = $out | append . -}} {{- end -}} {{- end -}} {{- end -}} {{- return $out -}}
